# No Fake Savings Policy

This lab must not claim real cost savings.

## Allowed Statements

- A proposed call was blocked by a deterministic budget gate.
- A fallback tool was executed after a primary call was blocked.
- The report charged a deterministic estimate supplied by the task.
- The run stayed within the configured deterministic budget.

## Disallowed Statements

- Claims that the implementation saved money in production.
- Percent savings, ROI, payback periods, or avoided invoice amounts.
- Claims based on invented telemetry, benchmarks, or usage volume.
- Provider-specific billing claims without real billing data.

## Reporting Standard

Every report includes a notice that estimates are task-provided and are not real-world billing or savings claims.
